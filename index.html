The EWF Innovation & Skills Academy Approach

Learn, Practise, Build & Apply.

The Academy’s approach connects concepts with practical exercises and relevant applications, as appropriate to each course and training arrangement.

Learn

Explore the concepts, tools, and technology behind a skill.

Practise

Work through exercises, workflows, and relevant tasks.

Build

Where included, apply your knowledge through projects, prototypes, or digital solutions.

Apply

Consider how the skills may support work, business, innovation, or personal projects.

Frequently Asked Questions

What to know before you enquire.

For accurate, up-to-date details about a particular training arrangement, contact EWF Innovation & Skills Academy directly.

What is EWF Innovation & Skills Academy?
EWF Innovation & Skills Academy is a technology and innovation skills-development initiative connected to the EWorldfeeders Global Link ecosystem. The website introduces training areas and provides a direct enquiry route for current course details.
What training areas are available?
The catalogue covers AI, software and web development, business systems, digital marketing, ICT infrastructure, AgroTech, renewable energy, Web3, and related subjects. Contact the Academy to confirm whether your chosen area is currently available.
How can I obtain the curriculum, duration, and fees?
Use the WhatsApp contact link on this page and mention your chosen course. The Academy can advise on the current curriculum, requirements, duration, delivery options, fees, and next steps.
Are training sessions online or in person?
Delivery options depend on the course and training arrangement. Please confirm the available format directly before registering.
Do I need previous experience?
Prerequisites vary by training area. Tell the Academy which course interests you and describe your experience so the applicable requirements can be clarified.
How do I register?
Begin by enquiring about your chosen course. Once the details are confirmed, follow the Academy’s registration instructions.
Are certificates available?
Certificate availability and completion requirements depend on the training arrangement. Please confirm the details before enrolling.
Can organisations request training?
Organisations and businesses can contact EWF Innovation & Skills Academy to discuss their training needs. Any customised programme, including its scope, schedule, and fees, must be discussed and confirmed.
